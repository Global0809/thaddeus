## Platform

## Product Purpose
A single-piece Thaddeus jewellery showcase and campaign, using the user's nine supplied photographs. Preserve the approved continuous AICANFEEL V5 native 3D journey while translating its garment and sports semantics into jewellery, gemstone light and campaign imagery.

## Brand Commitments
Thaddeus is the supplied brand name. Deep petrol, luminous teal, emerald green, polished gold and cool white reflections come from the campaign. The ring visible in the photographs is the hero design. The procedural geometric ring is a stylised visual interpretation, not a certified specification or exact CAD reconstruction.

## Current Capabilities

- Three connected perspectives on one ring, with native selector, camera approach, drag/touch interaction and photographic campaign release.
- Same-document ring details, nine-image gallery, saved selection, menu, privacy information and ring-measurement aid.
- Local enquiry preparation with save, copy, text-download and clear actions. No remote form submission.
- Personalised native artwork with square, landscape and portrait PNG downloads. No automatic social posting.
- Explicit sound opt-in with deferred audio loading, reduced-motion support, keyboard focus management and browser-storage fallbacks.
- WebGL preflight and loss/load-failure recovery that retain access to ring details and campaign imagery.

## Constraints

Preserve the approved garment-based AICANFEEL V5 checkpoint and its separate deployment. Reuse the native Hydra renderer, selector, scene transitions and input model. Keep the nine campaign photographs visually unaltered apart from responsive resizing/compression. Keep development direction and review notes outside browser-delivered files.

Price, gemstone/metal certification, stone provenance, exact sizing, stock, contact destination and purchasing integration remain unconfirmed. Do not invent these facts, brand history, scarcity or customer proof. Product copy calls enquiries local drafts and states that nothing is sent.
