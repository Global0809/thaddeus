# Thaddeus — the light room

The user's campaign is the visual authority: luminous turquoise, deep petrol, emerald facets, polished gold and cool white reflections. The existing continuous 3D interaction is the structural authority. No ordinary stacked shop template.

The first viewport presents the same ring in three viewpoints. The stone, the setting and the presence are chapters of one piece, not three invented products. The camera approaches the stone and setting as the visitor scrolls, then releases into a full-screen campaign photograph. Details and gallery use a same-document overlay so the visitor keeps their place.

Cormorant Garamond carries the engraved, high-contrast display language; DIN remains quiet for controls and detail. Paper-white text, pale teal secondary text, gold actions and a petrol studio ground hold across every state. Headlines remain short; the ring and campaign carry the identity.

Desktop uses empty space to the left of the ring. Mobile raises and condenses text, reduces geometry detail and separates the ring from the copy. Reduced motion holds the jewellery pose. Native rendering owns animation; no second renderer or timeline.

The setting, materials and faceting of the procedural ring are a visual interpretation of the photograph. Prices, gemstone certification, metal purity, availability and buying/contact destinations are not invented. Enquiries are explicitly local drafts.
