# THADDEUS — A rare kind of presence

Immersive jewellery brand preview using the supplied Thaddeus campaign images and a procedural native 3D interpretation of the green-centre ring.

Public website: https://global0809.github.io/thaddeus/

## Experience

Three connected perspectives — the stone, the setting and the presence — share the existing selector, touch and camera journey. Scroll to inspect the ring and reveal the campaign. Piece details, nine-image campaign gallery, saved selection and enquiry draft remain on the same page.

The ring is real geometry rendered by the native Hydra runtime, with a faceted green centre, stone halo, baguettes, gold-tone band and gallery. Five merged mesh draws per instance, shared geometry and reduced mobile detail. Reflections and gemstone light are stylised studio shader approximations.

## Product truth

The design and palette are interpreted from the supplied photographs. No price, gemstone/metal certification, product provenance, contact destination or purchasing service is asserted. The enquiry form explicitly saves a local draft and sends nothing. Sound requires visitor opt-in; saved selection and draft use browser localStorage only.

## Preservation

The prior approved garment-based AICANFEEL V5 remains in Git history and the tag `aicanfeel-v5-before-thaddeus`. The original Sites deployment and source archives are preserved separately.

## Files

`index.html`, `thaddeus.css`, `thaddeus.js`: brand interface and interactions. `energy-core.js`: the jewellery mesh and shader module (compatibility filename). `assets/js/app.js`: native renderer and guarded jewellery integration. `immersive-data.js`: narrative and native experience data. `thaddeus/media`: responsive campaign WebP images and source manifest. `thaddeus/fonts`: Cormorant Garamond and OFL licence.

GitHub Pages serves this branch directly with `.nojekyll`; asset URLs resolve against `/thaddeus/`.
