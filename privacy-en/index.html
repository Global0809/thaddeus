Privacy.

This AICANFEEL course preview does not collect payment or use advertising pixels. Course interactions and campaign parameters are held in browser memory for this visit; they are not sent to an AICANFEEL analytics server.

Instagram enquiries

Course enquiries open Instagram. Any message you choose to send is handled by Instagram and AICANFEEL through that service. Opening this preview does not send a message.

The saved worlds use local browser storage for experience preferences. A name entered for a personalized visual is rendered in your browser. The preview does not send your name or generated image to an AICANFEEL server.

The hosting provider handles standard connection and access information required to deliver the website. This site is publicly accessible without signing in.
